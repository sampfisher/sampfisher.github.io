What is left?

The encroachment of AI into so many aspects of our live is something, that on a purely personal level, I find very depressing. It feels like we are outsourcing our minds to a toaster (not my expression) and embracing the the Wall-E dystopia. I am aware it can write emails faster and probably do many of the boring aspects of our jobs for us. Rather than "free our minds" for the more interesting parts of life however, in practice we are just spending more time talking to the toaster. Worst of all however, is that it is not just the boring stuff that we use it for, we prompt into existence stories, visual 'art', music, designs, films, animations, and now of course mathematical theorems. And then we attach our names to these things, often use this to gain creadit, as if they were our creations. We are slowly losing the meaning of what 'creating' something is, as well as the appreciation of the skill and craft involved in creativity.
